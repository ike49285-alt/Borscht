    How to play
    • Everyone starts with 7 cards. Get rid of all of yours first.
    • Play a card that matches the bowl's color or its number or symbol. Playable cards lift up with a ring.
    • Four colors: Beet, Dill, Carrot and Cabbage.
    • ⊘ Skip jumps the next player. ⇄ Reverse flips the direction. +2 makes the next player draw 2 and lose their turn.
    • Smetana (wild) goes on anything and you pick the color. Smetana +4 also makes the next player draw 4 and lose their turn.
    • Can't play? Draw one. If it fits you may play it, otherwise your turn passes.
    • When you are about to play your second-last card, press Borscht! first. Forget, and you draw 2.
