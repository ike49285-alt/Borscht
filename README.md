# Borscht

A shedding card game in the family of Uno, for 2 to 4 players. Each seat can be a person or a computer. People share one device and pass it around.

Open `index.html` in a browser to play. There's nothing to build or install.

## Playing with friends

Choose **New game…** to set the number of players and pick a person or a computer for each seat. With two or more people, a cover screen hides every hand between turns. Pass the device, and the next player taps to show their cards. The game log only says that someone drew a card, never which card.

## Rules

- Each player starts with 7 cards. The first to empty their hand wins.
- Match the top card's color (Beet, Dill, Carrot, Cabbage) or its number or symbol.
- Skip, Reverse (a Skip when only two play) and +2 work as you'd expect. Smetana (wild) goes on anything and you pick the color. Smetana +4 also makes the next player draw 4 and lose their turn.
- If you can't play, draw one card. If it fits you may play it. Otherwise your turn passes.
- Press **Borscht!** before you play your second-last card. If you forget, you draw 2.
