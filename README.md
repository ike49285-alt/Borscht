# Borscht

A shedding card game in the family of Uno. You play against three computer opponents in the browser.

Open `index.html` in a browser to play. There's nothing to build or install.

## Rules

- Each player starts with 7 cards. The first to empty their hand wins.
- Match the top card's color (Beet, Dill, Carrot, Cabbage) or its number or symbol.
- Skip, Reverse and +2 work as you'd expect. Smetana (wild) goes on anything and you pick the color. Smetana +4 also makes the next player draw 4 and lose their turn.
- If you can't play, draw one card. If it fits you may play it. Otherwise your turn passes.
- Press **Borscht!** before you play your second-last card. If you forget, you draw 2.
